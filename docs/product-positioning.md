# ClawKB Product Positioning

## One-line Positioning

ClawKB is a personal, local-first knowledge base workbench for a single user who wants to store, search, read, and write against their own material without depending on a cloud service.

## Product Promise

- Your knowledge stays on your machine by default.
- The product is optimized for one person, not teams.
- AI is used to help you ask, read, summarize, draft, and organize what you already own.
- The product should feel simpler and calmer than a collaboration platform.

## Primary User

The primary user is an individual knowledge worker who wants:

- a private local knowledge base
- fast search and retrieval
- a workbench for reading and writing against imported material
- a credible local alternative to cloud-heavy knowledge assistants

## Non-goals

ClawKB is not currently positioned as:

- a multi-user collaboration suite
- a cloud workspace
- a team document hub
- a marketplace or social knowledge-sharing platform

## UX Principles

- Start with the workbench, not an admin dashboard.
- Keep navigation focused on the core loop: ask, choose library, open document, continue work.
- Make local ownership obvious in the product language.
- Avoid placeholder collaboration language that implies missing cloud features are around the corner.
